# Sofi Eureka! — Animation Brief

## Context

This spritesheet is used in **Ask Sofi** as a transition between the moment Sofi finishes processing a message and the moment she begins presenting a useful interpretation.

The intention is not to represent the discovery of an "absolute truth", but rather that moment when **Sofi connects the dots, finds something interesting, and gets ready to explain it to the user**.

The animation should feel **positive, clear, and charming**, reinforcing the idea that Sofi has something valuable to share while maintaining a friendly, lighthearted, and never arrogant tone.

## What the Animation Should Convey

The animation should communicate a progression similar to this:

- Sofi is finishing her thought process.
- She realizes she may have found something interesting.
- She experiences a small moment of excitement and satisfaction.
- She composes herself and gets ready to speak.
- The result should transition naturally into the existing **Talking** spritesheet.

The overall feeling should be:

- Discovery
- Clarity
- Gentle excitement
- Warmth
- Friendly confidence

It should **not** convey:

- Superiority
- Excessive drama
- Chaotic excitement
- The impression that Sofi knows the absolute truth about the relationship or situation

## Creative Freedom

You have **complete creative freedom** to build the best possible animation using the available materials.

You may:

- Reuse any existing frames or assets that improve the result.
- Take advantage of previous spritesheets, especially **Sofi Blink**, **Sofinking**, and **Talking**, whenever useful.
- Choose the final number of animation steps.
- Adjust pacing, holds, and transitions.
- Add subtle movement or visual effects.
- Use only the assets that genuinely contribute to the final animation.

The intention is more important than following a predetermined sequence.

The finished result should:

1. Feel consistent with Sofi's personality.
2. Maintain visual continuity with the existing spritesheets.
3. Work naturally as a transition into **Talking**.
4. Make Sofi's discovery feel useful, exciting, and approachable.

## Visual Style and Tone

Preserve Sofi's established pixel-art identity, including her two-tone hair, purple eyes, pink sweater, and choker.

Keep the artwork crisp, with clean transparency and consistent visual alignment.

The animation should feel alive without becoming visually overwhelming.

Eureka should be **cute and expressive, not grandiose or overly triumphant**.

Avoid embedding dialogue, captions, or interface elements directly into the sprites.

The application will handle the actual analysis content and conversational flow.


## Final Goal

Create a polished, charming, and expressive animation representing the moment when **Sofi discovers a useful interpretation and transitions naturally from thinking to explaining it**.

Prioritize emotional clarity, visual continuity, and the best possible final result over any rigid choreography or technical prescription.
