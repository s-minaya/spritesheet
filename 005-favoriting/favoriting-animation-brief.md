# Favoriting Animation Brief

## Purpose
This animation is the **favoriting / saved response** microinteraction for **Ask Sofi**.

Its role is to communicate that Sofi has marked something as approved / saved / favorited, with a playful, confident, over-the-top attitude that fits her established pixel-art identity.

The agent has freedom to implement the animation in the way that feels best in the UI, as long as it preserves the personality, readability, and pixel-art feel of the provided frames.

---

## Assets 
1. **Base / setup**  
   Sofi facing the user with a soft open-mouth expression.

2. **Glasses down**  
   Sofi lowers / wears the sunglasses with a smug, confident expression.

3. **Glasses shine**  
   Same pose and sunglasses, now with the sparkle highlight on the glasses.

4. **Stamp asset**  
   Independent `SOFI APPROVED` stamp asset, to be animated separately from Sofi.

---

The overall feeling should be:
- readable
- satisfying
- slightly dramatic / diva-like
- cute, polished, and expressive
