# Sofi Suspicious — Animation Brief

## Purpose in Ask Sofi

`sofi-suspicious` is a subtle character reaction for **Feature 008 — Sofi Character & Motion**. It accompanies an interpretation in which the analysis has **sufficient evidence of possible sarcasm or an indirect meaning**.

The goal is to make Sofi feel perceptive, a little mischievous, and conversational—as if she has noticed an interesting nuance between the lines. **She is not judging the partner, assigning blame, or declaring an interpretation to be certain.**

The app decides whether this animation is appropriate based on the actual analysis result. A short message such as **“Vale.”** must never trigger this reaction merely because of its wording or punctuation. Do not use it to suggest manipulation, guilt, a red flag, or any risk that the result does not substantiate. Sensitive and safety-related states require their own restrained presentation.

## Available artwork and intended expressions

The character images are **already created**. Use the supplied, approved/corrected assets as source material; there is no need to redraw them. The descriptions below explain their emotional purpose, **not a mandatory playback order**.

- **F1 — Neutral / Blink (existing asset):** Sofi's normal, relaxed expression. A natural starting point or transition that can be reused from the existing `sofi-blink` spritesheet.
- **F2 — Hmm... interesting:** `suspicious-01` Her eyes glance sideways, one eyebrow lifts, one eye narrows slightly, and her mouth barely curves. This is the first hint that she has noticed a subtle nuance, without reaching a conclusion.
- **F3 — I see what you did there...:** `suspicious-02` The sideways glance becomes a small, asymmetric, knowing smile. Sofi looks playful and almost conspiratorial, but **never mocking, malicious, or superior**. This is the main expressive beat.
- **F4 — Let me explain...:** `suspicious-03` Sofi looks directly at the user again, her eyebrows relax, and she offers a warm, open smile. This expression prepares the handoff to the existing **Talking** spritesheet so the interpretation can be presented naturally.

Use the latest corrected transparent versions where applicable. Any extra effects are optional; the expression itself should carry the personality.

## Creative freedom and repository task

Create the finished **spritesheet**, following its existing asset, metadata, and preview conventions. Inspect what the repo already provides and reuse it rather than introducing a second animation framework.

You have **full creative freedom** to choose the best images, pacing, movement, transitions, holds, reuse of `sofi-blink` or other compatible sprites, and the final structure. You may omit redundant material or refine the choreography if it improves the result. Do not treat the four descriptions as a rigid timeline.

Preserve Sofi's established pixel-art identity: crisp edges and transparency, split dark/blonde hair, purple eyes, pink sweater, and choker. Keep the result subtle, easy to interrupt, and visually compatible with the handoff to **Talking**.

Do **not** embed dialogue, analysis claims, captions, or interface elements into the artwork. The main Ask Sofi app owns the analysis, state selection, text, and transitions; this repository provides the visual animation.

**Priority:** A fleeting, cute, perceptive side-eye that smoothly becomes an approachable explanation—not a verdict about anyone's intentions.
