# Sofi Uncertain — Spritesheet Brief

## Purpose
Sofi Uncertain represents Sofi gracefully acknowledging uncertainty or missing information. It can accompany answers such as “I don't know” or “It depends” during onboarding, and moments when the user cannot provide further context during an analysis.

Its purpose is to make uncertainty feel accepted rather than problematic. Sofi responds with warmth and understanding, without pressuring the user, inventing conclusions, or suggesting certainty that the available evidence cannot support.

## Tone and Intent
The overall feeling should be:
- warm
- understanding
- honest
- slightly playful / cute
- emotionally safe

Sofi should come across as someone who recognizes uncertainty gracefully. The animation should avoid:
- blame
- guilt
- frustration
- sarcasm
- indifference
- overly dramatic confusion

## Creative Freedom
You have **full creative freedom** to build the best possible spritesheet for this state.

You can:
- reuse any existing Sofi assets that help
- reuse elements from the **neutral** or **blink** spritesheets if useful
- adjust timing, spacing, transitions, and frame order as needed
- refine the poses or facial micro-expressions if that improves the final result

The goal is not to follow the source frames rigidly, but to create the **best final animation** that conveys the intended feeling and works naturally inside the product.

## Source Images and What They Represent
Use the available images as intent references for the state.

### 1. Neutral / baseline Sofi
This is the calm starting point. It represents Sofi before acknowledging uncertainty. We can reuse sofi-blink and/or listening.

### 2. “uncertain-01”
This image represents the beginning of uncertainty.

Intent:
- Sofi starts a **small shrug**
- her shoulders begin to rise a little
- her head tilts slightly
- her expression becomes gently unsure
- she is still engaged with the user

This should feel like a soft preparation for: *we may not have enough information*.

### 3. “uncertain-02?”
This is the main expressive frame of the state.

Intent:
- Sofi completes a **small shrug**
- the hands appear near the body, slightly open, with palms up
- the expression is cute, shy, and understanding
- she is clearly admitting uncertainty, but in an affectionate and natural way

This is the core visual moment of the animation.

### 4. “That’s okay.”
This is the recovery / return frame.

Intent:
- Sofi lowers the shrug
- the hands leave the frame again
- her face becomes calm and warm
- she returns to a relaxed, reassuring expression

This frame should communicate acceptance, closeness, and emotional safety. We can reuse blink in here.

## Animation Goal
The final animation should feel like a short emotional beat:
1. Sofi realizes there is not enough context
2. she acknowledges that uncertainty gently
3. she reassures the user
4. she settles back into a calm, friendly state

It should feel natural in conversation, as if asking for more context has ended and Sofi is now ready to continue without pretending to know more than she does.

## Implementation Notes
- Prioritize **readability, clarity, and charm** over strict fidelity to every source pose.
- Preserve Sofi’s established pixel-art identity, including the recognizable face, split hair, and choker.
- If any existing frame from another spritesheet gives a better transition, feel free to reuse it.

## Summary
This animation should make uncertainty feel:
- honest
- gentle
- emotionally intelligent
- cute
- safe

Sofi is not giving up. She is simply acknowledging, in a warm and human way, that sometimes **we just can’t know for sure**.
